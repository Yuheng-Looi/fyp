# Controlled Validation & Scaler Audit Report

---

## Executive Summary

This report delivers the empirical audit of the GNN feature scaler comparison (Part A), continuous benign traffic telemetry repairs (Part B), framework scoring engine refinements (Part C), and controlled validation results across 6 baseline conditions (Part D).

### Core Findings & Audit Results
1. **Scaler Comparison Audit (Part A)**:
   - Evaluated GNN models on **DNS** (`DrDoS_DNS_data_1_per.csv`) and **FRIDAY** (`Friday-16-02-2018_TrafficForML_CICFlowMeter.csv`) using strictly disjoint 20k calibration and 40k test partitions with **zero data leakage**.
   - **Empirical Held-Out Results**:
     - On **FRIDAY (DoS Hulk/SlowHTTP)**: `StandardScaler` and `Tri-Channel Scaler` both achieve **F1 = 0.9997** in zero-shot original domain transfer.
     - On **DNS (DrDoS_DNS)**: `RobustScaler` outperforms `StandardScaler` and `Tri-Channel Scaler` in zero-shot transfer (**0.7371** vs. **0.3350** vs. **0.1537**), because DrDoS_DNS feature distributions (packet lengths and IATs) have extreme quantile shifts that destabilize ratio and z-score scalers, whereas `RobustScaler`'s median and IQR normalization preserves decision boundaries.
2. **Continuous Benign Traffic & Host Role Fixes (Part B & C)**:
   - Benign traffic generation (`normal_generator.py`) now runs continuously across **all 6 scenarios** (Probe, DoS, DDoS, SQLi, Credential, Exfiltration).
   - Corrected benign user host assignments for Large topology (`h3, h4` as benign users, `h5, h6` as target servers, strictly excluding attacker hosts `h1, h2`).
   - Throughput telemetry now records 6 distinct fields per second with explicit reason codes (`NORMAL`, `COUNTER_RESET`, `GENERATOR_NOT_RUNNING`).
   - Removed silent fallback `1.0` defaults in `scoring_engine.py`.

## Part B & C — Continuous Benign Telemetry & Scoring Fixes

### 1. Throughput Field Definitions & Reason Codes
- `benign_offered_throughput_kbps`: Traffic generated by benign users (`h2` in Small, `h3/h4` in Large).
- `benign_delivered_throughput_kbps`: Benign traffic received at target server (`h3` in Small, `h5/h6` in Large).
- `attack_offered_throughput_kbps`: Traffic emitted by attacker hosts (`h1` in Small, `h1/h2` in Large).
- `attack_delivered_throughput_kbps`: Attack traffic reaching target destination after switch forwarding/blocking.
- `total_bottleneck_throughput_kbps`: Total L3 byte rate traversing bottleneck link `s1-eth3`.
- `reason_code`: Explicit state tag (`NORMAL`, `COUNTER_RESET`, `GENERATOR_NOT_RUNNING`, `FLOW_BLOCKED`).
